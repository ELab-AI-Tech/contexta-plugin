# Contexta for Claude

Contexta keeps your company brain, agent skills and workflows current in every AI your team uses. Your admins set your company context, link the knowledge base that stays in your own SharePoint or Google Drive, and write the skills for the jobs your team repeats. This plugin brings them into Claude, so the work comes back the way your business does it.

## What You Can Do

- Find the skills your organization has and use the one that fits the task.
- Follow a skill's steps, format and checks.
- Apply your company context: who you are, what you sell, your terminology and naming.
- Work within company, team and personal spaces.
- See a skill's version history and what changed.
- Save new draft skills and update existing ones, if you're an admin or editor.

## What You Need

- A Contexta account. Start at [getcontexta.ai](https://getcontexta.ai).
- Claude with plugin support (Claude, Cowork or Claude Code).

## Set Up

1. Install the Contexta plugin from the Claude directory.
2. Connect Contexta when Claude asks, and sign in to Contexta. Contexta uses OAuth, so there's no API key to paste.
3. Say **set up Contexta** to set up your workspace, or ask Claude to use one of your organization's skills.

To check the connection, ask Claude which Contexta organization it's connected to.

## Included Skills

| Skill | Use it when | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `contexta` | You ask to use, find or follow one of your organization's skills | Finds the matching skill in your Contexta library and applies it to the task |
| `setup` | "Set up Contexta" | Connects this workspace to your Contexta space and creates its folders, with your confirmation at each step |
| `faq` | You ask how Contexta works | Answers from the Contexta help content |
| `audit` | You ask Claude to check this workspace for overlapping skills | Reports overlaps between local skills and your Contexta skills, without changing anything |

## Privacy and Data

Contexta stores your skills, company context and account details. Your documents stay in your own storage. Read the [Privacy Policy](https://getcontexta.ai/privacy) and [Security and Data](https://getcontexta.ai/security).
